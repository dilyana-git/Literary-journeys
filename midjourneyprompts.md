## 1. The Odyssey (Homer, ~8th c. BCE)

### Troy
```
Ancient walled city of Troy burning at dawn, Greek warships beached on a sandy shore, smoke rising from crumbling towers, a helmeted warrior looking back from the stern of a departing galley, Aegean sea glittering beyond
```

### Ismarus (the Cicones)
```
Greek warriors raiding a coastal Thracian village, thatched huts ablaze, amphorae of wine spilling on the ground, angry spearmen counterattacking from the hills, a beached trireme with oars stacked, wild coastline
```

### Land of the Lotus-Eaters
```
Drowsy sailors lying among enormous flowering lotus plants on a North African shore, one man being dragged back to a ship by comrades, languid tropical atmosphere, date palms and white sand dunes, a beached galley waiting
```

### Island of the Cyclopes
```
A towering one-eyed giant Polyphemus hurling a boulder at a fleeing Greek ship, inside a cave with enormous sheep, a sharpened olive-wood stake smouldering on the ground, rocky Sicilian coastline, volcanic cliffs
```

### Aeolia
```
A floating island palace surrounded by bronze walls, the wind god Aeolus handing a leather bag tied with silver cord to a bearded Greek captain, swirling wind patterns in the sky, volcanic Aeolian islands in the background
```

### Land of the Laestrygonians
```
Giant cannibals hurling boulders from clifftops into a narrow harbour, smashing Greek ships below, terrified sailors swimming, a single ship escaping through the harbour mouth, towering Sardinian coastal cliffs, chaos and destruction
```

### Aeaea (Circe's island)
```
An enchantress in flowing robes standing at the door of a stone palace, men transformed into swine rooting in the courtyard, one hero holding a drawn sword and a magical herb, dense Mediterranean forest, wild boar and deer
```

### The Underworld
```
A bearded hero kneeling at the edge of a dark pit, ghostly translucent shades rising from black blood pooled on the ground, the blind prophet Tiresias among the dead, a misty volcanic landscape, Lake Avernus steaming
```

### The Sirens
```
A Greek ship passing a rocky islet, the captain lashed to the mast straining toward two beautiful singers on the rocks, crew rowing with wax plugged ears, bones and wrecked ships scattered on the reef, turbulent Mediterranean waves
```

### Scylla & Charybdis
```
A six-headed sea serpent Scylla snatching sailors from a ship's deck while a massive whirlpool Charybdis churns on the opposite side, the Strait of Messina narrowing between cliffs, desperate helmsman steering through, spray and terror
```

### Thrinacia
```
Starving Greek sailors slaughtering magnificent golden cattle on a Sicilian hillside, the sun god's sacred herd, storm clouds gathering ominously overhead, one man trying to stop the others, divine wrath approaching
```

### Ogygia
```
A beautiful nymph and a melancholy bearded man sitting in a lush island grotto covered in flowering vines, the man gazing out at the empty sea, a hidden paradise with waterfalls and singing birds, Malta's golden limestone cliffs
```

### Scheria
```
A shipwrecked man crawling ashore on a white beach, a young princess with handmaidens doing laundry by a river mouth, a gleaming Phaeacian palace in the distance, olive groves and Corfu's green hills, dawn light
```

### Ithaca
```
A disguised beggar drawing a mighty bow in a columned hall, suitors feasting at long tables unaware, a faithful old dog lying at the threshold, a rocky Greek island visible through the doorway, homecoming and reckoning
```

## 2. On the Road (Jack Kerouac, 1957)

### New York City
```
A young man with a rucksack standing at a Manhattan street corner in 1949, yellow cabs and steam rising from manhole covers, neon signs for jazz clubs, brownstone tenements, the Brooklyn Bridge in the distance, autumn leaves blowing
```

### Chicago
```
A smoky 1940s Chicago jazz club interior, a saxophone player on a small stage, Beat generation figures in rolled-up sleeves listening intently, beer bottles on small tables, exposed brick and tin ceiling, the L train visible through a window
```

### Des Moines
```
A man asleep on a bench at a lonely Iowa bus station at dawn, flat prairie stretching to the horizon outside, a Greyhound bus idling, empty coffee cup and scattered dimes, existential stillness, grain silos in the morning mist
```

### Denver
```
A group of young bohemians on a Denver front porch at night, beer and cigarettes, the Rocky Mountains silhouetted under stars, old cars parked on a tree-lined street, a wild-haired figure talking with manic energy, 1940s clothing
```

### Bakersfield / Central Valley
```
Migrant cotton pickers in a vast California field under blazing sun, a young man and a Mexican woman picking side by side, tents and jalopy trucks at the field's edge, San Joaquin Valley flatlands, dust and heat haze
```

### San Francisco
```
A 1940s San Francisco street scene, cable car climbing a steep hill, fog rolling through the Golden Gate, neon-lit North Beach jazz bars, two friends walking with collars up, Victorian row houses and the Bay shimmering below
```

### New Orleans (Algiers)
```
A ramshackle house across the Mississippi from New Orleans, a strange intellectual surrounded by guns and books on the porch, steamboats and the French Quarter skyline visible across the river, Spanish moss and humid air, 1940s
```

### Detroit
```
A desolate 1940s Detroit skid row at night, a man sleeping in a doorway near the railroad tracks, factory smokestacks and automobile plants in the background, empty bottles and newspapers, cold streetlight, industrial grime
```

### Mexico City
```
A fevered young American lying in a cheap Mexico City hotel room, ceiling fan turning slowly, Aztec and colonial architecture visible through an open balcony door, a friend departing in a battered car below, tropical plants, 1950
```

## 3. Journey to the End of the Night (Céline, 1932)

### Paris
```
A young man in a shabby suit watching a military regiment march down a 1914 Parisian boulevard, crowds waving flags, café tables on the sidewalk, Haussmann buildings, a recruitment poster on the wall, naive patriotic fervour about to shatter
```

### The Western Front
```
A shell-blasted WWI trench in Flanders, mud and barbed wire, exhausted soldiers huddled against sandbag walls, rain pouring, an explosion lighting the horizon, rats and scattered equipment, crosshatch shading creating deep oppressive shadows
```

### Bambola-Bragamance
```
A fever-racked European man at a decrepit colonial trading post deep in equatorial Africa, thatched warehouse collapsing, jungle pressing in, a muddy river with a canoe, corrupt officials drinking on a veranda, oppressive heat rendered in dense linework
```

### New York City
```
A lone European immigrant arriving at the Manhattan waterfront in the 1920s, towering skyscrapers viewed from below, crowds and traffic overwhelming, the Statue of Liberty behind, steamship smokestacks at the dock, isolation amid the spectacle
```

### Detroit
```
Workers on a 1920s Ford automobile assembly line, repetitive mechanical movements, deafening machinery drawn with precise mechanical crosshatching, a dazed European among the workers, factory windows showing smokestacks, dehumanising industrial rhythm
```

### Rancy
```
A doctor making a house call in a grimy Parisian working-class suburb at night, narrow streets with peeling walls, a sick child in a cramped room, the doctor carrying a worn leather bag, gaslight and deep shadows, poverty and resignation
```

### Toulouse
```
A tense scene in a bourgeois Toulouse parlour, lace curtains and dark furniture, an old woman in a chair, a sinister conversation, the pink-brick city visible through a window, moral corruption hidden behind respectability
```

### Vigny-sur-Seine
```
A run-down asylum on the banks of the Seine at dusk, a doctor standing at the iron gates looking out at the river, patients wandering the grounds, bare trees, fog rolling in, the end of all journeys, melancholy and resignation
```

## 4. Avignon Quintet (Lawrence Durrell, 1974–85)

### Avignon
```
The Palais des Papes in Avignon at sunset, massive medieval fortress-palace walls glowing gold, the Pont d'Avignon broken in the Rhône, Templar symbols carved in stone, cypress trees, lavender-scented Provençal air, literary mystery
```

### Verfeuille / Tu Duc
```
A crumbling Provençal country chateau in overgrown grounds, shuttered windows and climbing roses, a group of intellectuals and lovers gathered on a stone terrace with wine, cicadas, olive groves and distant purple hills
```

### Geneva
```
Wartime Geneva in winter, a psychoanalyst's office with leather couch and bookshelves overlooking Lake Geneva, snow on the Jet d'Eau, Swiss neutrality and hidden tensions, a woman staring out the window at the Alps, cerebral atmosphere
```

### Venice
```
A masked figure in a black cloak stepping from a gondola at a crumbling Venetian palazzo, carnival masks hanging in a window, reflections rippling in canal water, Bridge of Sighs in the background, decadence and intrigue, winter fog
```

### Alexandria
```
The ancient lighthouse of Alexandria reimagined, a harbour with feluccas and colonial-era buildings, a European writer wandering the Corniche, minarets and palm trees, Egyptian sun blazing on white buildings, Templar treasure map in hand
```

### Cairo & the Desert
```
An expedition of Europeans on camels approaching the Great Pyramids at Giza at dawn, the Sphinx half-buried in sand, treasure-hunting equipment and maps, a vast empty desert stretching behind, dust and golden light, archaeological adventure
```

### Vienna
```
A pre-war Viennese café with marble-topped tables and gilt mirrors, Freud's portrait on the wall, intellectuals debating over newspapers and coffee, the Ringstrasse visible through the window, chandeliers, psychoanalysis and foreboding, 1930s
```

## 5. Moby-Dick (Herman Melville, 1851)

### New Bedford
```
A young sailor with a sea-bag entering a dark whaling inn at night, a harpooner covered in tattoos sleeping in the same bed, whale-oil lamps and harpoons mounted on the wall, cobblestone whaling port, Massachusetts winter fog
```

### Nantucket
```
The whaling ship Pequod departing Nantucket harbour on Christmas morning, three masts rigged with square sails, ice on the rigging, a peg-legged captain silhouetted on the quarterdeck, grey Atlantic waves, small island town receding behind
```

### Cape of Good Hope
```
A whaling ship rounding the Cape of Good Hope in heavy seas, enormous waves breaking over the bow, albatrosses soaring overhead, the rocky southern tip of Africa in storm clouds, sailors clinging to the rigging, wild Southern Ocean
```

### Indian Ocean
```
Whaleboats lowered from a ship hunting sperm whales in tropical Indian Ocean waters, a massive whale spouting, harpoons flying, turquoise sea and white clouds, the ship Pequod watching from the background, blood staining the water
```

### Strait of Sunda
```
Hundreds of sperm whales surging through a narrow tropical strait between Java and Sumatra, whaleboats caught in the stampede, volcanic islands on both sides, jungle coastline, a grand armada of whales stretching to the horizon, chaos
```

### The Pacific
```
A great white whale smashing a whaling ship to splinters in the open Pacific Ocean, the whale's scarred head ramming the hull, sailors thrown into the water, masts falling, a lone coffin floating amid wreckage, apocalyptic sky and sea
```

## 6. Around the World in 80 Days (Jules Verne, 1873)

### London (Departure)
```
A precise English gentleman in top hat and frock coat descending the steps of the Reform Club on Pall Mall, a loyal French valet scrambling behind with luggage, a hansom cab waiting, Big Ben showing the exact hour, Victorian London fog
```

### Paris
```
A steam locomotive crossing the Channel on a ferry then racing through the French countryside, the Gare du Nord in Paris with iron-and-glass roof, a gentleman checking his pocket watch, 1870s Parisian boulevards visible, steam and urgency
```

### Brindisi
```
A train emerging from an Alpine tunnel into sunny southern Italy, the port of Brindisi with a P&O steamer waiting at the dock, Mediterranean blue water, Roman columns and Italian architecture, a gentleman rushing from train to ship, luggage porters
```

### Suez
```
The Suez Canal with a steamship passing between sandy desert banks, a detective in a bowler hat watching from the shore with a warrant, Egyptian buildings and minarets, camels on the towpath, the Red Sea glinting beyond, suspicion and pursuit
```

### Bombay
```
A steamship arriving in 1870s Bombay harbour, the Gateway of India area, a French valet accidentally entering a Hindu temple with his shoes on, British colonial architecture, elephants and bustling bazaar, tropical heat
```

### Calcutta
```
An elephant carrying three European travellers and an Indian guide through dense Indian jungle, a young woman being rescued from a funeral pyre in a forest clearing, tigers watching from the undergrowth, temple ruins, dramatic rescue scene
```

### Hong Kong
```
A Victorian-era Hong Kong harbour full of junks and steamships, a detective slipping opium into a Frenchman's drink in a dockside tavern, lanterns reflecting in the water, the Peak rising behind, fog and conspiracy, 1870s colonial waterfront
```

### Yokohama
```
A French valet performing acrobatics in a Japanese circus tent, balancing on a human pyramid while wearing traditional Japanese costume, a Victorian gentleman spotting him from the audience, 1870s Yokohama harbour with steamships outside
```

### San Francisco
```
A 1870s San Francisco street with cable cars, a Victorian gentleman caught in a rowdy American political rally, fistfights and top hats flying, Gold Rush-era wooden buildings, a steam train waiting at the depot, western energy and chaos
```

### New York
```
A desperate Victorian gentleman at the New York docks finding the steamer to Liverpool has just departed, smokestacks vanishing on the horizon, the Brooklyn Bridge under construction, horse-drawn carriages, racing against time, 1870s
```

### London (Return)
```
A gentleman bursting through the doors of the Reform Club at the last second, pocket watch in hand, astonished club members rising from leather chairs, the clock striking the final hour, gaslight and cigar smoke, triumphant Victorian moment
```

## 7. Don Quixote (Cervantes, 1605–15)

### La Mancha
```
A gaunt elderly man in rusty ill-fitting armour mounting a bony horse in the courtyard of a dusty Spanish farmhouse, a stout peasant on a donkey watching skeptically, the flat arid plains of La Mancha stretching to the horizon, windmills distant
```

### Campo de Criptana
```
A knight on a thin horse charging lance-first at a row of whitewashed windmills on a Spanish hilltop, the windmill sails spinning, his squire on a donkey shouting warnings, blue sky and red earth, the moment of impact, comic and heroic
```

### Puerto Lápice
```
A run-down roadside Spanish inn that a deluded knight sees as a grand castle, the innkeeper "knighting" him with a sword, prostitutes giggling in the doorway as ladies-in-waiting, a donkey drinking from a trough, La Mancha dust and heat
```

### Sierra Morena
```
A half-naked knight performing penitence on a rocky mountain ledge in the Sierra Morena, beating his head against rocks, his squire watching in dismay from below, wild goats on the cliffs, Spanish mountain wilderness, imitating a romantic hero
```

### Cave of Montesinos
```
A knight being lowered on a rope into a deep dark cave, magical visions inside of enchanted knights and ladies frozen in crystal, underground lake and stalactites, his squire holding the rope at the entrance above, dreamlike underworld
```

### Barcelona
```
A knight in battered armour defeated on a Barcelona beach by a rival knight in gleaming white, the Mediterranean Sea behind them, the city's Gothic spires rising, spectators watching, a broken lance on the sand, the end of chivalric dreams
```

### Home
```
An old man lying in bed in a simple Spanish village house, surrounded by weeping friends, a pile of chivalric romances burning in the fireplace, armour and lance hung on the wall, the man lucid and peaceful at last, La Mancha light through the window
```

## 8. Heart of Darkness (Joseph Conrad, 1899)

```

```

### Brussels
```
A young seaman entering a gloomy Continental office building, two women knitting black wool in the waiting room, a doctor measuring his skull, maps on the wall with a large blank space marked Congo, bureaucratic dread, 1890s Brussels grey streets
```

### Mouth of the Congo
```
A steamship approaching the mouth of the Congo River from the Atlantic, dense mangrove coastline, surf crashing on a sandbar, a French warship firing into the empty jungle, absurdity and menace, West African tropical coast, heat haze
```

### Company Station
```
A desolate colonial station carved from the jungle, chain gangs of African labourers carrying baskets, rusting machinery abandoned in the grass, a grove where sick workers crawl away to die, a white accountant in immaculate clothes at a desk, horror
```

### Central Station

A steamboat sunk in a muddy river at a ramshackle colonial station, a European manager scheming on a veranda, ivory tusks stacked in a shed, the jungle pressing in from all sides, repairs being made with rivets, Congo River brown and vast

### Inner Station
```

```

```
A young sailor in a white shirt being arrested on his wedding day at a sunny Marseille harbour, gendarmes seizing him at the altar, his bride screaming, a felucca moored at the quay, then the dark cell of the Château d'If fortress on its rocky island
```

### Monte Cristo
```
A man emerging from the sea at night onto a wild rocky Mediterranean island, climbing to a hidden cave filled with gold coins and jewels and ancient treasure chests, torchlight gleaming on gemstones, the transformation from prisoner to count, rebirth
```

### Rome
```
A masked nobleman at the Roman Carnival, confetti and flower-throwing from balconies, a kidnapping by bandits in the Colosseum ruins at night, the Count rescuing a young Frenchman in a torchlit bandit cave, masks and daggers, 1830s Rome
```

### Paris
```
A mysterious wealthy count arriving at a grand Parisian mansion in a magnificent carriage, Parisian high society peering from behind curtains, the Champs-Elysées lined with gas lamps, a man playing chess with his enemies' fates, 1830s elegance and revenge
```

### Marseille (Departure)
```
A cloaked figure standing at the stern of a yacht sailing away from Marseille at dawn, the Château d'If prison fortress visible on its island behind, the city's harbour and Notre-Dame de la Garde basilica on the hill, justice completed, the open sea ahead
```
